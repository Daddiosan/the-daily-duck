Scientists are turning moon dust into LEGO-like bricks to help build future lunar bases!

Today's Story

Researchers have found a way to transform moon dust into building bricks that click together much like LEGO blocks. This creative technique could help construct habitats for future lunar bases using materials already present on the Moon.
