NASA IMAP releases science data from 7 instruments

Today's Story

All 10 science instruments aboard NASA's IMAP mission are successfully collecting data. On August 7, NASA released science data gathered through April 30 from seven of those instruments. IMAP studies the solar wind, interstellar particles and dust to help us understand the environment surrounding our solar system.

The Duck

A day when we got to peek a little farther into the unknown. So today's duck is a curious little astronaut — small body, very big view.

NASA IMAP、7機器の科学データを公開
