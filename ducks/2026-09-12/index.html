BUZZING BEE

Bite, buzz, release: How bees make flowers give up their pollen
One day. One story. One duck.

Ever wonder how bees shake pollen free from flowers? They use a clever technique: bite, buzz with rapid muscle vibrations, and release!

Today's Story

Nature has some clever tricks, and bees have mastered a fascinating one for gathering pollen. By biting into flowers and using rapid muscle vibrations, they shake the pollen free in a method known as 'bite, buzz, release.' It is a wonderful example of tiny creatures using smart techniques to get their work done.

The Duck

Quack! Bite, buzz, release—bees sure know how to get things moving!
