ROCK-THROWING CHIMP DUCK

One day. One story. One duck.

West African chimpanzees have been throwing rocks at the exact same trees for over ten years, creating mysterious rock piles! A fascinating look into animal behavior.

Today's Story

Researchers studying West African chimpanzees have uncovered a fascinating ritual: the primates have been hurling stones at the exact same trees for more than ten years. Over time, these repeated actions have created mysterious piles of rocks near the trees. While scientists continue to study the purpose behind this intriguing behavior, it offers a captivating glimpse into animal traditions and curiosity.
