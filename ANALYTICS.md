# Google Analytics 4

Measurement ID: G-ZY7DDS7PRO

Version 3.8 installs the Google tag on all HTML pages currently in the site.

After deployment:
1. Open https://www.thedailyduck.ai/
2. In Google Analytics, use "Test installation" / インストールをテスト.
3. Then check Realtime to confirm your visit appears.

No Google Tag Manager container is required for this setup.
